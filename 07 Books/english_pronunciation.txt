English Pronunciation (উচ্চারণ) - Details

English pronunciation means how English words are spoken correctly. It
is different from spelling.

1.  English Sounds Vowels: A, E, I, O, U Examples: cat -> /æ/ bed -> /e/
    sit -> /ɪ/ hot -> /ɒ/ cup -> /ʌ/

Consonants: b, c, d, f, g, h etc.

2.  IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet) It shows exact pronunciation.
    car -> /kɑːr/ cinema -> /ˈsɪnəmə/

3.  Why pronunciation is confusing English spelling and sound are often
    different.

Example: cinema -> /ˈsɪnəmə/

4.  c rule c + a/o/u = /k/ (cat, cup) c + e/i/y = /s/ (city, cent)

5.  Silent letters knife (k silent) hour (h silent)

6.  Stress Some syllables are pronounced with more stress: CIN-e-ma
    a-BOUT

7.  Summary English pronunciation is a sound system, not spelling based.
    Practice and listening are important.
