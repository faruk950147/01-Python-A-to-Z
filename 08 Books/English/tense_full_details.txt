# Tense (Full Details)

Tense হলো verb-এর এমন একটি form যা কাজের সময় বোঝায়।

ইংরেজিতে প্রধানত ৩ ধরনের tense আছে:

1. Present Tense (বর্তমান)
2. Past Tense (অতীত)
3. Future Tense (ভবিষ্যৎ)

প্রতিটি আবার ৪ ভাগে বিভক্ত।

মোট = 12 Tense

==================================================

12 Tense Chart

Present:
- Present Simple
- Present Continuous
- Present Perfect
- Present Perfect Continuous

Past:
- Past Simple
- Past Continuous
- Past Perfect
- Past Perfect Continuous

Future:
- Future Simple
- Future Continuous
- Future Perfect
- Future Perfect Continuous

==================================================

1. Present Indefinite / Simple Present

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + Verb1 + Object

Negative:
Subject + do/does not + Verb1

Question:
Do/Does + Subject + Verb1?

Uses:
- Habit
- Universal truth
- Daily routine

Examples:
- I play football.
- She goes to school.
- The sun rises in the east.

==================================================

2. Present Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + am/is/are + Verb+ing

Negative:
Subject + am/is/are not + Verb+ing

Question:
Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- এখন কাজ চলছে

Examples:
- I am reading.
- They are playing.
- She is cooking.

==================================================

3. Present Perfect

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + have/has + Verb3

Negative:
Subject + have/has not + Verb3

Question:
Have/Has + Subject + Verb3?

Uses:
- কাজ শেষ হয়েছে
- Experience

Examples:
- I have completed the work.
- She has gone home.

==================================================

4. Present Perfect Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + have/has been + Verb+ing + since/for

Negative:
Subject + have/has not been + Verb+ing

Question:
Have/Has + Subject + been + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- কাজ কিছু সময় ধরে চলছে

Examples:
- I have been studying for two hours.
- She has been working since morning.

==================================================

5. Past Indefinite / Simple Past

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + Verb2

Negative:
Subject + did not + Verb1

Question:
Did + Subject + Verb1?

Uses:
- অতীতে কাজ হয়েছে

Examples:
- I played cricket.
- She went home.

==================================================

6. Past Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + was/were + Verb+ing

Negative:
Subject + was/were not + Verb+ing

Question:
Was/Were + Subject + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- অতীতে কোনো কাজ চলছিল

Examples:
- I was reading.
- They were playing.

==================================================

7. Past Perfect

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + had + Verb3

Negative:
Subject + had not + Verb3

Question:
Had + Subject + Verb3?

Uses:
- অতীতে একটি কাজ আরেকটির আগে শেষ হয়েছিল

Examples:
- I had finished the work before he came.
- She had left before sunset.

==================================================

8. Past Perfect Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + had been + Verb+ing + since/for

Negative:
Subject + had not been + Verb+ing

Question:
Had + Subject + been + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- অতীতে কিছু সময় ধরে কাজ চলছিল

Examples:
- I had been studying for three hours.
- They had been playing since morning.

==================================================

9. Future Indefinite / Simple Future

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + will + Verb1

Negative:
Subject + will not + Verb1

Question:
Will + Subject + Verb1?

Uses:
- ভবিষ্যতে কাজ হবে

Examples:
- I will go there.
- She will help me.

==================================================

10. Future Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + will be + Verb+ing

Negative:
Subject + will not be + Verb+ing

Question:
Will + Subject + be + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- ভবিষ্যতে কোনো কাজ চলতে থাকবে

Examples:
- I will be studying.
- They will be traveling.

==================================================

11. Future Perfect

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + will have + Verb3

Negative:
Subject + will not have + Verb3

Question:
Will + Subject + have + Verb3?

Uses:
- ভবিষ্যতে নির্দিষ্ট সময়ের আগে কাজ শেষ হবে

Examples:
- I will have completed the work.
- She will have arrived by 5 PM.

==================================================

12. Future Perfect Continuous

Structure:
Positive:
Subject + will have been + Verb+ing + since/for

Negative:
Subject + will not have been + Verb+ing

Question:
Will + Subject + have been + Verb+ing?

Uses:
- ভবিষ্যতে কিছু সময় ধরে কাজ চলতে থাকবে

Examples:
- I will have been studying for two hours.
- They will have been working since morning.

==================================================

Verb Forms

Verb1 = go
Verb2 = went
Verb3 = gone
Verb+ing = going

==================================================

Since vs For

Since:
- since 2020
- since morning

For:
- for 2 years
- for 5 hours

==================================================

Shortcut Formula

Simple = Verb
Continuous = be + ing
Perfect = have + V3
Perfect Continuous = have been + ing
