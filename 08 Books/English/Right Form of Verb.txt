# Right Form of Verb

Right Form of Verb means using the correct form of a verb according to the tense, subject, and structure of a sentence.

Example:

He go to school. ❌
He goes to school. ✅

They goes to school. ❌
They go to school. ✅


# 1. Present Indefinite / Simple Present

Structure:

I / We / You / They + V1

He / She / It + V1 + s/es

Examples:

I play football.
We play football.
They play football.

He plays football.
She watches TV.
Rahim goes to school.


# 2. After Modal Verbs

After can, could, may, might, shall, should, will, would, must, etc. → V1

Examples:

He can swim. ✅
He can swims. ❌

You should study. ✅
You should studies. ❌

She will come. ✅
She will comes. ❌

They must obey the rules. ✅


# 3. After "To"

Usually:

to + V1

Examples:

I want to learn Python.
She wants to go home.
He decided to study.
They came to help me.


# 4. After Prepositions

Preposition + V-ing

Examples:

He is interested in learning Python.
She is good at drawing.
Thank you for helping me.
I am tired of waiting.


# 5. Present Continuous

Structure:

am / is / are + V-ing

Examples:

I am reading.
He is playing.
She is cooking.
They are studying.


# 6. Past Continuous

Structure:

was / were + V-ing

Examples:

I was reading.
He was sleeping.
They were playing.
We were studying.


# 7. Present Perfect

Structure:

has / have + V3

Examples:

I have finished my work.
They have gone home.
He has eaten lunch.
She has completed the task.


# 8. Past Perfect

Structure:

had + V3

Examples:

I had finished the work.
He had gone home.
They had eaten dinner.


# 9. Future

Structure:

will + V1

Examples:

I will go.
He will come.
They will study.
She will help me.


# 10. Passive Voice

Structure:

be + V3

Examples:

The work is done.
The letter was written.
The book is written by him.
The car was repaired.


# 11. Have / Has / Had

Have / Has / Had + V3

Examples:

I have eaten.
He has eaten.
They have eaten.
She had eaten.


# 12. Do / Does / Did

After do / does / did → V1

Examples:

I do not like tea.
He does not like tea.
They did not go there.

Does he play football?
Did you see him?

NOT:

He does not likes tea. ❌
He does not like tea. ✅

Did you went there? ❌
Did you go there? ✅


# 13. "Let"

Let + object + V1

Examples:

Let me go.
Let him play.
Let them come.
Let us help you.


# 14. Make

Make + object + V1

Examples:

The teacher made me study.
My mother made me clean the room.


# 15. Had Better

had better + V1

Examples:

You had better study.
You had better go home.
He had better be careful.


# 16. Would Rather

would rather + V1

Examples:

I would rather stay home.
She would rather walk.
He would rather study.


# 17. Used To

used to + V1

Used to = past habit

Examples:

I used to play football.
He used to live in Dhaka.
They used to go there.


# 18. Be Used To

be used to + V-ing / noun

Meaning: অভ্যস্ত হওয়া

Examples:

I am used to working hard.
She is used to getting up early.
He is used to living alone.


# 19. There Is / There Are

There is + singular noun
There are + plural noun

Examples:

There is a book on the table.
There are two books on the table.


# 20. If Condition

## Type 1

If + Present Simple, will + V1

Examples:

If you study hard, you will pass.
If it rains, we will stay home.


## Type 2

If + Past Simple, would + V1

Examples:

If I had money, I would buy a car.
If I were rich, I would help people.


## Type 3

If + had + V3, would have + V3

Examples:

If I had studied, I would have passed.
If he had come, I would have helped him.


# Important Verb Forms

V1 = Base Form
V2 = Past Form
V3 = Past Participle
V4 = -ing Form
V5 = s/es Form


Example:

GO

V1 = go
V2 = went
V3 = gone
V4 = going
V5 = goes


EAT

V1 = eat
V2 = ate
V3 = eaten
V4 = eating
V5 = eats


WRITE

V1 = write
V2 = wrote
V3 = written
V4 = writing
V5 = writes


DO

V1 = do
V2 = did
V3 = done
V4 = doing
V5 = does


# Quick Rules

Simple Present:
He/She/It → V5
I/We/You/They → V1

Modal → V1

To → V1

Preposition → V-ing

am/is/are → V-ing

was/were → V-ing

has/have/had → V3

will → V1

do/does/did → V1

be → V3 (Passive Voice)

let → V1

make → V1

had better → V1

would rather → V1

used to → V1


# Common Mistakes

He go to school. ❌
He goes to school. ✅

He can goes. ❌
He can go. ✅

She did not went. ❌
She did not go. ✅

I have saw him. ❌
I have seen him. ✅

He is play football. ❌
He is playing football. ✅

I am interested in learn Python. ❌
I am interested in learning Python. ✅
