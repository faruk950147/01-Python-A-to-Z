# TENSES — MASTER CLASS

Tense tells us WHEN an action happens.

There are 3 main times:

1. Present → বর্তমান
2. Past → অতীত
3. Future → ভবিষ্যৎ

Each time has 4 forms:

1. Simple
2. Continuous
3. Perfect
4. Perfect Continuous

So, total:

3 × 4 = 12 Tenses


==================================================
1. PRESENT TENSE
==================================================


# 1. Present Simple / Simple Present

Use:
- Habit
- Routine
- General truth
- Fact
- Permanent situation

Structure:

I/We/You/They + V1

He/She/It + V5

Examples:

I play football.
আমি ফুটবল খেলি।

He plays football.
সে ফুটবল খেলে।

They go to school.
তারা স্কুলে যায়।

She goes to school.
সে স্কুলে যায়。


Negative:

I/We/You/They + do not + V1

He/She/It + does not + V1

Examples:

I do not play football.

He does not play football.


Question:

Do + I/we/you/they + V1?

Does + he/she/it + V1?

Examples:

Do you play football?

Does he play football?


Important:

Does + V1

He does not goes. ❌
He does not go. ✅

Does he plays? ❌
Does he play? ✅


Common time expressions:

always
usually
often
sometimes
every day
every week
never


Example:

I usually wake up at 6 AM.

She always drinks tea.


--------------------------------------------------


# 2. Present Continuous

Use:
- Action happening now
- Temporary action
- Changing situation
- Near future arrangement

Structure:

Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

Examples:

I am reading.

He is reading.

They are reading.


Negative:

Subject + am/is/are + not + V-ing

Examples:

I am not sleeping.

He is not working.

They are not playing.


Question:

Am/Is/Are + Subject + V-ing?

Examples:

Are you studying?

Is he sleeping?

Are they playing?


Time expressions:

now
right now
at the moment
currently
these days


Example:

I am studying English now.

She is working at the moment.


--------------------------------------------------


# 3. Present Perfect

Use:
- Completed action with present result
- Life experience
- Recently completed action
- Action that happened at an unspecified time

Structure:

Subject + has/have + V3

I/We/You/They → have

He/She/It → has

Examples:

I have finished my work.

He has finished his work.

They have eaten lunch.

She has gone home.


Negative:

Subject + has/have + not + V3

Examples:

I have not finished.

He has not arrived.


Question:

Has/Have + Subject + V3?

Examples:

Have you finished?

Has he arrived?


Common words:

already
just
yet
ever
never
recently
so far
before


Examples:

I have already finished my work.

She has just arrived.

Have you ever visited Dhaka?

I have never seen this movie.


Important:

Present Perfect সাধারণত specific finished time-এর সাথে ব্যবহার করা হয় না।

I have seen him yesterday. ❌

I saw him yesterday. ✅


--------------------------------------------------


# 4. Present Perfect Continuous

Use:
- Action started in the past and is still continuing
- Duration of an ongoing action

Structure:

Subject + has/have + been + V-ing

Examples:

I have been studying for two hours.

He has been working since morning.

They have been playing for three hours.


Negative:

I have not been studying.

He has not been working.


Question:

Have you been studying?

Has he been working?


Common words:

for
since
all day
all morning
lately
recently


FOR = duration

for two hours
for three days
for five years


SINCE = starting point

since 2020
since Monday
since morning
since 5 PM


==================================================
2. PAST TENSE
==================================================


# 5. Past Simple / Simple Past

Use:
- Completed action in the past
- Past habit
- Series of past actions

Structure:

Subject + V2

Examples:

I went to school.

He played football.

They watched a movie.


Negative:

Subject + did not + V1

Examples:

I did not go.

He did not play.

They did not watch.


Question:

Did + Subject + V1?

Examples:

Did you go?

Did he play?

Did they watch?


IMPORTANT:

After DID → V1

He did not went. ❌
He did not go. ✅

Did you saw him? ❌
Did you see him? ✅


Common time expressions:

yesterday
last night
last week
last year
ago
in 2020
when I was young


Examples:

I visited Dhaka yesterday.

He graduated in 2020.

She called me two hours ago.


--------------------------------------------------


# 6. Past Continuous

Use:
- Action happening at a specific time in the past
- Interrupted past action
- Two actions happening simultaneously

Structure:

Subject + was/were + V-ing

I/He/She/It → was

We/You/They → were


Examples:

I was studying.

He was sleeping.

They were playing.


Interrupted action:

I was sleeping when he called me.

She was cooking when I arrived.


Two simultaneous actions:

While I was studying, my brother was watching TV.


Negative:

I was not sleeping.

They were not playing.


Question:

Was he sleeping?

Were they playing?


--------------------------------------------------


# 7. Past Perfect

Use:

An action that happened BEFORE another past action.

Structure:

Subject + had + V3

Examples:

I had finished my work before he arrived.

She had left before I came.

They had eaten before the movie started.


Timeline:

Past Perfect              Past Simple
     ↓                          ↓
I had eaten           →    He arrived.


Example:

When I arrived, he had already left.


Negative:

Subject + had not + V3

Example:

He had not finished the work.


Question:

Had + Subject + V3?

Example:

Had he finished the work?


Common words:

before
after
already
just
by the time
until then


--------------------------------------------------


# 8. Past Perfect Continuous

Use:

An action continued for a period of time before another past action.

Structure:

Subject + had + been + V-ing

Examples:

I had been studying for two hours before he came.

She had been working since morning before she went home.

They had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived.


Timeline:

Started
  ↓
[ Continuous Action ]
  ↓
Past event


Example:

I had been waiting for two hours when the bus arrived.

3. FUTURE TENSE
==================================================


# 9. Future Simple

Use:
- Future decision
- Prediction
- Promise
- Offer
- Future action

Structure:

Subject + will + V1

Examples:

I will go.

He will come.

They will help us.


Negative:

Subject + will not + V1

Examples:

I will not go.

He will not come.


Question:

Will + Subject + V1?

Examples:

Will you come?

Will he help me?


Common expressions:

tomorrow
next week
next year
soon
later
in the future


Examples:

I will call you tomorrow.

She will come soon.


--------------------------------------------------


# 10. Future Continuous

Use:

An action that will be happening at a specific time in the future.

Structure:

Subject + will be + V-ing

Examples:

I will be studying at 8 PM.

He will be working tomorrow morning.

They will be traveling next week.


Negative:

I will not be studying.

He will not be working.


Question:

Will you be studying?

Will he be working?


Example:

At 10 PM tonight, I will be sleeping.


--------------------------------------------------


# 11. Future Perfect

Use:

An action that will be completed before a specific future time.

Structure:

Subject + will have + V3

Examples:

I will have finished the work by 5 PM.

She will have arrived by tomorrow.

They will have completed the project by next week.


Timeline:

NOW
 ↓
Action
 ↓
Completed
 ↓
Future deadline


Example:

By next year, I will have completed my degree.


Common words:

by
by then
by tomorrow
by next year
before


--------------------------------------------------


# 12. Future Perfect Continuous

Use:

An action that will continue for a duration up to a specific future time.

Structure:

Subject + will have been + V-ing

Examples:

By next month, I will have been working here for two years.

By 10 PM, she will have been studying for five hours.

Next year, he will have been living here for ten years.


Timeline:

NOW
 ↓
Continuous action
 ↓
Future point

==================================================
KEY DIFFERENCES
==================================================


# Simple vs Continuous

Simple:
I study English.
→ Habit / general action

Continuous:
I am studying English.
→ Happening now


# Simple Past vs Present Perfect

Past Simple:
I saw him yesterday.
→ Specific finished past time

Present Perfect:
I have seen him.
→ Experience / unspecified time


# Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Perfect:
I have read the book.
→ Focus on completion/result

Present Perfect Continuous:
I have been reading the book for two hours.
→ Focus on duration/activity


# Past Simple vs Past Perfect

Past Simple:
He arrived after I left.

Past Perfect:
He arrived after I had left.

Past Perfect makes the earlier action clearer.


# Future Simple vs Future Continuous

Future Simple:
I will study at 8 PM.
→ Future action

Future Continuous:
I will be studying at 8 PM.
→ Action will be in progress at that time.


# Future Perfect vs Future Perfect Continuous

Future Perfect:
I will have completed the work by 5 PM.
→ Completion

Future Perfect Continuous:
I will have been working for five hours by 5 PM.
→ Duration


==================================================
SINCE vs FOR
==================================================

SINCE = Starting point

since 2020
since Monday
since morning
since 5 PM


FOR = Duration

for two hours
for three days
for five years
for ten minutes


Examples:

I have lived here since 2020.

I have lived here for six years.


==================================================
WHEN vs WHILE
==================================================

WHEN is often used with a shorter action/event.

I was sleeping when he called.


WHILE is often used with an ongoing action.

While I was sleeping, he called.


Another example:

I was studying when my friend arrived.

While I was studying, my friend was watching TV.


==================================================
BY vs UNTIL
==================================================

BY = Before or no later than a deadline.

Finish the work by 5 PM.

Meaning:
5 PM-এর মধ্যে কাজ শেষ করতে হবে.


UNTIL = Up to a particular time.

I will wait until 5 PM.

Meaning:
5 PM পর্যন্ত অপেক্ষা করব.
