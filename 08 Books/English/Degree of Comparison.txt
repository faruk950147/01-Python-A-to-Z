# Degree of Comparison

Degree of Comparison is used to compare people, animals, things, or qualities.

There are 3 types of Degree:

1. Positive Degree
2. Comparative Degree
3. Superlative Degree


# 1. Positive Degree

Positive Degree describes a quality without making a comparison.

Structure:

Subject + am/is/are + adjective

Examples:

Rahim is tall.
Karim is intelligent.
The flower is beautiful.


# 2. Comparative Degree

Comparative Degree is used to compare two people or things.

Usually:

Adjective + er + than

or

more + adjective + than

Examples:

Rahim is taller than Karim.
This book is more interesting than that book.


# 3. Superlative Degree

Superlative Degree is used to compare three or more people or things and show the highest degree of a quality.

Usually:

the + adjective + est

or

the most + adjective

Examples:

Rahim is the tallest boy in the class.
This is the most interesting book.


# Basic Comparison

Positive       Comparative       Superlative

tall            taller             tallest
small           smaller            smallest
fast            faster             fastest
short           shorter            shortest
long            longer             longest


# Adjectives Ending in "e"

Add:

r → Comparative
st → Superlative

Positive       Comparative       Superlative

large           larger             largest
nice            nicer              nicest
wise            wiser              wisest
brave           braver             bravest


# Short Adjectives Ending in Consonant + Vowel + Consonant

Double the final consonant.

Positive       Comparative       Superlative

big             bigger             biggest
hot             hotter             hottest
thin            thinner            thinnest
fat             fatter             fattest


# Adjectives Ending in "y"

Change y → i and add er/est.

Positive       Comparative       Superlative

easy            easier             easiest
happy           happier            happiest
busy            busier             busiest
heavy           heavier            heaviest


# Long Adjectives

Use:

more + adjective
most + adjective

Positive            Comparative             Superlative

beautiful            more beautiful          most beautiful
difficult             more difficult           most difficult
important             more important          most important
expensive             more expensive          most expensive
interesting           more interesting        most interesting


# Irregular Degrees

Some adjectives have completely different forms.

Positive       Comparative       Superlative

good            better             best
bad             worse              worst
little          less               least
much            more               most
many            more               most
far             farther/further    farthest/furthest
old             older/elder        oldest/eldest


# Examples

## Good

Positive:
He is a good student.

Comparative:
He is better than me.

Superlative:
He is the best student in the class.


## Bad

Positive:
This is a bad road.

Comparative:
This road is worse than that road.

Superlative:
This is the worst road in the area.

# Comparative Sentence

Comparative সাধারণত দুইজন/দুইটি জিনিসের মধ্যে comparison করে।

Structure:

A + is + Comparative + than + B

Examples:

Rahim is taller than Karim.

Dhaka is bigger than Narayanganj.

Python is easier than C++.


# Superlative Sentence

Superlative সাধারণত তিন বা তার বেশি ব্যক্তি/বস্তুর মধ্যে সর্বোচ্চ বা সর্বনিম্ন বোঝায়।

Structure:

Subject + is + the + Superlative + noun

Examples:

Rahim is the tallest boy in the class.

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.

This is the best book.


# Positive Degree for Equal Comparison

Positive Degree can also be used to show equality.

Structure:

as + adjective + as

Examples:

Rahim is as tall as Karim.

This book is as interesting as that book.

She is as intelligent as her sister.


# Negative Comparison

Structure:

not as + adjective + as

Examples:

Rahim is not as tall as Karim.

This book is not as interesting as that one.

C++ is not as easy as Python.


# Important Rules

1. Comparative Degree usually uses "than".

He is taller than me. ✅

2. Superlative Degree usually uses "the".

He is the tallest boy. ✅

3. Do not use "more" with a normal "-er" comparative.

more taller ❌
taller ✅

more faster ❌
faster ✅

4. Do not use "most" with a normal "-est" superlative.

most tallest ❌
the tallest ✅

most fastest ❌
the fastest ✅
